The Virgen del Carmen from the sea

Every 16 July the Virgen del Carmen is carried out to sea off El Palo and Pedregalejo. We take you to follow the procession from the water.

How the trip goes

We leave Real Club El Candado at 19:00, a few minutes from El Palo, and follow the sea procession among the boats that accompany it. We are back in port at about 22:00.

We always stay outside any area closed off that day by the Harbour Master and the organisers.

There is only one boat, so this trip can be booked once, for a group of up to 8 people.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the sea procession?

Off El Palo and Pedregalejo, on Málaga’s eastern coast, very close to El Candado.

Can we get close to the Virgin?

We sail alongside the boats that follow the procession, always outside the area set by the Harbour Master and the organisers.

What if we cannot go out that day?

If bad weather or restrictions stop the trip, you get the full deposit back.

How do I pay?

When you book you pay a €20 per person deposit. The rest in cash on the day.
