Sunset boat trip off Málaga

We leave in time for a swim and come back as the city lights up behind us. The departure time follows each day’s sunset.

What the sunset trip is like

We board two and a half hours before sunset: around 19:00 in midsummer and about 15:30 in winter. When you book, you see the exact time for the day you pick.

We swim in the afternoon light in a cove towards Peñón del Cuervo and, as the sun goes down, anchor off the bay to watch it with Málaga behind. We head back to port with the city lit up.

It is the trip most often booked for anniversaries and proposals. For a proposal there is a pack with flowers, cava, decoration and photos (€120) that you add when booking.

Frequently asked questions

What time do we leave?

Two and a half hours before that day’s sunset: around 19:00 in summer and about 15:30 in winter. The exact time appears when you pick the day.

Is it good for a proposal?

Yes, it is the trip most often booked for that. When booking you can add the proposal pack (flowers, cava, decoration and photos) for €120.

Does it get cold on the way back?

It gets cooler at sea once the sun goes down, even in summer. Bring a light layer.

Do we need a boating licence?

No. The boat is always run by the skipper, who is her owner. You just enjoy yourselves.

What if the weather is bad?

The skipper decides whether it is safe to go out, based on the forecast and the sea. If a trip is cancelled because of the weather, you choose between another date or a full refund of the deposit.

How do I pay?

When you book you pay a €20 per person deposit online, which comes off the total. The rest is paid in cash on the day, before boarding.
