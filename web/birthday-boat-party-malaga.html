A birthday on a boat along the Málaga coast

A boat just for your people, with a skipper, a swim in a cove and the cake on deck. For up to 8 people of any age.

A birthday your way

You set the party up however you like: cake, cava, music or snacks. The boat has a fridge and cool box with ice, a bimini for shade, a sun deck on the bow and a full bathroom on board.

Children of any age are welcome: there are life jackets in every size and children always come with an adult. If you would rather blow out the candles as the sun goes down, the sunset trip costs the same and its time follows the season.

If you would rather find it ready, you can add balloons and a banner (€30), a bottle of cava with glasses (€40), artisan Málaga ice cream (€18) or a cheese and Iberian ham board (€15 per person) when booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can children come?

Yes, any age. There are life jackets for all ages and children always come with a responsible adult.

Can we bring a cake and food?

Yes. You can bring your own food and drinks; the boat has a cool box with ice.

Can I give the trip as a present?

Yes. With the gift voucher you pay online, receive it straight away and the recipient picks the date within 6 months.

Can the dog come?

Yes, pets are welcome on board. Message the skipper on WhatsApp first to tell him the size and whether it gets seasick.

What if the weather is bad?

The skipper decides whether it is safe to go out, based on the forecast and the sea. If a trip is cancelled because of the weather, you choose between another date or a full refund of the deposit.

How do I pay?

When you book you pay a €20 per person deposit online, which comes off the total. The rest is paid in cash on the day, before boarding.
